- embed or bundle Attome (or its engine, CLI or daemon) in a product you distribute without releasing that product under the AGPL;
- offer a modified Attome as a hosted or network service without publishing your changes;
- want to keep your modifications private while distributing them;
- need terms the AGPL does not give you (warranty, indemnity, support, a different license on contributions).

## How

Open an issue titled "Commercial license" on this repository, or use the contact listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Say what you want to build and roughly how large your company is. Pricing is by agreement and scales with company size; individuals and small teams doing legitimate work are never the target.
